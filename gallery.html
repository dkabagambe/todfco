Bwaise Flood Sensitization: Listening to the Community

TODFCO held a community conference and outreach program in Bwaise, educating residents about floods, flood preparedness, and prevention measures. The team conducted flood inspections and documented the lived experiences and challenges of the people of Bwaise — giving voice to a community that has long borne the burden of recurring floods.
